Why It Exists

Making early detection easier

Alzheimer’s is hard to detect early. This platform provides simple tools using MRI images and clinical data to give a quick initial prediction.

─ It is an academic project, and results are not a medical diagnosis.

Core Capabilities

What This Website Offers

A structured, two-track analytical system designed to give clinicians and researchers a structured preliminary view of neurological health indicators.

01
MRI Image Processing

Uploads are processed through grayscale conversion and Otsu thresholding before being passed to a trained CNN for binary classification.

02
Clinical Data Analysis

Patient metadata — age, gender, MMSE score, CDR rating, and estimated total intracranial volume — is analysed using an ensemble classifier.

03
Self-Administered Assessment

A structured MMSE and CDR proxy questionnaire auto-scores orientation, memory, attention, language, and functional capacity.
